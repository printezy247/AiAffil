1What it does

A dedicated page lays out the major crypto exchanges, automated trading bots, and charting platforms side by side — fees, what each is best for, and a direct link to sign up. Instead of your audience searching around and landing on a competitor's referral link, they compare and choose right on your site.

2Why it matters

  • Captures high-intent traffic. Someone comparing exchanges is close to signing up — this is one of the highest-converting page types for affiliate revenue.
  • Positions you as the trusted source. A clear, organized comparison builds more credibility than a single "use this one" recommendation.
  • Covers multiple products per visitor. A trader might join an exchange, a trading bot, and a charting tool — three commission opportunities from one page view.
