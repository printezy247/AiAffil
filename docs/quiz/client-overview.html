1What it does

Instead of handing a visitor 165 tools and hoping they find the right one, the quiz asks what they're actually trying to accomplish and what their budget looks like, then hands back a short, relevant shortlist. It feels like a recommendation from someone who gets it, not a search results page.

2Why it matters

  • Higher conversion than browsing. A personalized recommendation converts better than a wall of options — people act faster when a decision has already been narrowed for them.
  • Lower bounce. Visitors overwhelmed by choice often leave without clicking anything; a guided path keeps them engaged.
  • Naturally promotes featured/priority tools. Results favor featured picks, giving you a built-in way to steer traffic toward your best-performing or highest-commission tools.
