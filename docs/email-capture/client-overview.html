1What it does

A visitor's site visit ends the moment they close the tab — unless you've captured a way to reach them again. This feature turns anonymous traffic into an owned email list, backed by a written welcome sequence that introduces new subscribers to the catalog over their first ten days.

2Why it matters

  • An email list is an asset you own. Unlike social platforms or search rankings, no algorithm change can take away access to your list.
  • Repeat exposure beats one visit. Most visitors won't buy or click on their first visit — an email sequence gives your recommendations multiple chances to land.
  • The hard part is already done. The five emails are written, sequenced, and ready — no staring at a blank page trying to figure out what to send first.
Ready to activate The signup form is live and collecting emails right now. Connecting a sending provider (10-minute setup) turns on full automatic delivery of the welcome sequence.
