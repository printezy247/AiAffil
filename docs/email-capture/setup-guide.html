Email Capture + Drip Sequence

Feature 13 of 13 — homepage signup form, plus a ready-to-use 5-email welcome sequence.

Works with zero setup ConvertKit integration built in 5-email sequence included

1What this does

A signup form on the homepage collects email addresses. Right now (no email provider connected), addresses are safely stored and viewable on your /admin/links dashboard — but no emails are actually sent until you connect a real provider. Connect ConvertKit and it starts working immediately.

Important: addresses aren't emailed to anyone automatically yet Without a connected provider, this only collects emails — it doesn't send anything. Follow step 2 below to actually start delivering the welcome sequence.

2Connect ConvertKit (recommended path)

STEP 1
Create a form
Sign up at convertkit.com (free up to 1,000 subscribers), create a form, note its Form ID from the form's settings/embed code.
STEP 2
Set 2 env vars
CONVERTKIT_API_KEY (Account Settings → Developer) and CONVERTKIT_FORM_ID, then redeploy.
STEP 3
Load the sequence
In ConvertKit, create an Automation triggered by that form, and add the 5 emails from docs/email-capture/drip-sequence.md with the suggested delays.

3Using a different provider

Using Mailchimp, IContact, or another provider instead? Edit the fallback block in app/api/subscribe/route.ts — swap the ConvertKit fetch call for that provider's API (most have a simple "add subscriber to list" REST endpoint). The email-collection form itself doesn't need any changes.

4The 5-email sequence

Already written and ready to paste in: docs/email-capture/drip-sequence.md. Covers a welcome email, a mindset/how-to-use-this-list email, a highlight of an underrated category, a plug for the free tools, and a wrap-up that invites replies and points to your Telegram channel.
