# 5-email welcome sequence

Copy these into your email provider (ConvertKit, Mailchimp, etc.) as an automation
triggered by the homepage signup form. Replace `{{name}}`/`{{email}}` with your
provider's actual merge tags, and swap any `/go/...` links for your own domain once
it's live. Suggested send delays are in the header of each email — adjust to taste.

---

## Email 1 — Welcome (send immediately)

**Subject:** You're in — here's where to start

Hey,

Thanks for joining. You now get one useful AI tool a week, picked from a
hand-curated list of 165+ — no fluff, no "50 tools you'll never use" list dumps.

To get started right now, here are 3 places worth a look:

- **[Browse the full catalog](/#tools)** — organized by what you're trying to do (video, writing, SEO, sales, and more)
- **[Take the 2-question quiz](/quiz)** — tells you exactly which tool fits your goal
- **[See today's featured picks](/)** — the tools we'd recommend first

Reply to this email any time — a real person reads these.

Talk soon,
{{sender_name}}

---

## Email 2 — Send 2 days later

**Subject:** The one mistake people make with AI tools

Most people sign up for an AI tool, poke at it for 10 minutes, and never come back —
not because the tool is bad, but because they never had a specific use case for it.

Here's the fix: pick *one* problem you actually have this week (writing faster,
editing video, following up with leads) and try exactly one tool against exactly
that problem. Don't browse the whole catalog looking for "the best one" — there
isn't one best tool, there's the right tool for what you're doing right now.

**[Take the quiz](/quiz)** if you want that decision made for you in about 15 seconds.

---

## Email 3 — Send 4 days later

**Subject:** A tool most people skip (and shouldn't)

Faceless video tools get a lot of attention because they're flashy, but the tools
people underrate are the boring, high-leverage ones — SEO and email tools compound
over months in a way a single video doesn't.

If you haven't looked at the **[SEO & Growth Marketing](/category/seo-and-growth-marketing)**
or **[Email & Newsletter](/category/email-newsletter-and-community)** categories yet,
that's worth five minutes this week.

---

## Email 4 — Send 7 days later

**Subject:** Free tools you're probably not using yet

Not everything here costs money. A good chunk of the catalog is completely free —
research tools, calculators, utilities that solve one specific annoying problem.

Two worth trying right now, no signup or payment required:
- **[Position Size Calculator](/tools/position-size-calculator)** — if you trade, this takes the guesswork out of how much to buy
- **[Live Crypto Prices](/markets)** — a quick market check without opening five tabs

---

## Email 5 — Send 10 days later

**Subject:** What's next

You've had this list for a week and a half now — if a tool from it has actually
made your week easier, hit reply and tell me which one. I read every response and
it directly shapes what gets added next.

If you're active on Telegram, [join the channel](#) for a daily tool pick + first
access to anything new before it hits the site.

That's the end of the welcome series — from here you'll just get the regular
weekly pick. Thanks for being here.

{{sender_name}}
