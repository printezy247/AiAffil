Auto-Generated "X vs Y" Comparison Pages

Feature 9 of 13 — 139 SEO pages generated automatically from your existing catalog data.

139 pages, zero manual writing No fabricated claims Auto-updates with your catalog

1What this does

For every category, up to 6 tools (featured ones first) are paired up into every combination, generating a dedicated page at /compare/tool-a-vs-tool-b for each pair — 139 pages total today. Each page shows both tools side by side using their real descriptions and affiliate status — nothing is invented or guessed about features neither tool's description actually mentions.

Why capped at 6 tools per category? Your largest category has 55 tools — pairing all of them would generate 1,485 pages just for that one category. Capping at 6 (featured tools prioritized) keeps the page count reasonable (139 total) while still covering your most important tools. Raise or lower MAX_TOOLS_PER_CATEGORY in lib/comparisons.ts if you want more or fewer.

2Nothing to configure

These pages regenerate automatically on every deploy based on whatever's in data/products.json — mark more tools "featured": true and they'll start appearing in more comparison pairs automatically.

3Where they're linked from

Each tool's own page (/tool/[slug]) shows "vs [other tool]" buttons for every comparison it's part of, so these pages aren't orphaned — they're discoverable from normal browsing, not just the sitemap.
