1What it does

Your audience taps a menu button right inside their chat with your bot — no browser, no app store, no friction — and your entire tool catalog opens in a smooth, native-feeling screen inside Telegram itself. It automatically matches whatever theme (light, dark, or custom colors) they already use in Telegram, and greets them by name.

3Why it matters

  • Removes the biggest drop-off point. Every extra tap, app switch, or page load loses people — this collapses "browse tools" into a single in-chat action.
  • Feels premium. Most Telegram channels only post links; offering an actual in-app experience sets your channel apart.
  • Meets your audience where they already are. If your audience lives in Telegram, this is the lowest-friction way to reach them — no separate website visit required.
