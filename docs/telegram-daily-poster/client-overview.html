1What it does

Every day, a short, engaging post goes out to your Telegram channel introducing one AI tool from your catalog — its name, what it does, and a "Visit" button. Tools rotate automatically so your audience always sees something fresh, and the whole catalog gets exposure over time without any manual scheduling.

2How it works

EACH MORNING
A tool is selected
The system rotates through your full tool list so nothing gets forgotten and nothing repeats too often.
AUTOMATICALLY
It's posted for you
A ready-to-read message with a clear "Visit" button goes out to your channel — no copywriting, no scheduling apps, no manual work.
EVERY CLICK
Is tracked
Every button click is recorded, so you can see which tools your audience actually cares about.

3Why it matters

  • Consistency without effort. Channels that post daily grow faster and stay top-of-mind — this runs whether or not you're online.
  • Full catalog exposure. Tools that would otherwise sit buried on page 3 of your website get their own moment in front of your audience.
  • Zero ongoing cost. It runs on free infrastructure — no subscription, no server bill.
  • Data-backed decisions. Click tracking shows exactly which tools resonate, so future promotion (and which affiliate programs to prioritize joining) is guided by real numbers, not guesswork.
Ready out of the box This is fully built and tested. Turning it on takes about 10 minutes and requires no technical background — just a few copy-and-paste steps with Telegram's official bot setup tool.
