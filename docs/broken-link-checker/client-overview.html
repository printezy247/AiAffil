1What it does

A broken affiliate link doesn't just look bad — it's a lost commission every time a visitor clicks it and hits a dead page. This runs a check on every single link in the catalog automatically once a week and flags any that stop working, so problems get caught before they cost money.

2Why it matters

  • Protects revenue silently. Companies rebrand, change URLs, or shut down — this catches it within a week instead of whenever you happen to notice.
  • Protects trust. A site full of dead links looks abandoned; visitors notice, even subconsciously.
  • Zero maintenance. Runs on a schedule automatically — nothing to remember to do.
